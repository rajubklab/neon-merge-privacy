Privacy Policy — Neon Merge

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Neon Merge ("the app") is a single-player number-merge puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data the app itself collects

None. The app has no accounts, no login, no analytics SDK, and no server of its own. Your best score, your game in progress and your sound preference are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. They are never transmitted anywhere.

Data collected by advertising

The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads). To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (AD_ID) and approximate, IP-derived location
  • device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • ad interaction events (an ad was shown, clicked, or a reward was earned)

This data is collected by Google, not by us. We never see it in a form that identifies you, and we do not sell or share it ourselves.

Google's handling of this data is governed by:

  • Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites and apps — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): on first launch the app shows Google's consent form, where you choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change that choice at any time via Privacy settings in the pause menu.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. They are only shown when you tap a button that says it will play an ad.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Ad requests are configured with a maximum content rating of T (Teen) and are not tagged as child-directed.

Permissions the app requests

Permission Why
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Load ads
AD_ID Serve and measure ads (Google AdMob)
VIBRATE Haptic feedback when a block lands

The app does not request the camera, microphone, contacts, files, precise location, or any other sensitive permission.

Data deletion

No data leaves your device except through Google's ad SDK, so uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. To remove ad-related data, reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date changes with it and the new version is published at the same URL.
